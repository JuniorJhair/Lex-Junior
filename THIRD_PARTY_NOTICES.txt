    brave-cookie-specific, brave-specific, brave-sugarcoat, coin-miners
  Licencia: Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0)
  Referencia: https://github.com/brave/adblock-lists/blob/master/LICENSE

- Declarative Surrogates & Scriptlets (adblock-resources / uAssets):

  bajo GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)
